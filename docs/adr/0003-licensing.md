# ADR 0003 — MIT code, reserved brand assets

**Status:** accepted · 2026-11-15

## Context

The project will be public on GitHub to build trust and community, and may be
sold later (open-core). A pure MIT license would give the brand away with the code.

## Decision

- Code: MIT ([LICENSE](../../LICENSE)).
- Name "Cortex", icon, and any future mascot/sounds: all rights reserved
  ([LICENSE-ASSETS.md](../../LICENSE-ASSETS.md)). Forks must rename and rebrand.

This mirrors a proven model (coucou, among many) and keeps the commercial door
open without closing the source.

## Consequences

- Community can read, audit, fork and contribute freely.
- The product identity remains ours; a future paid tier (team/enterprise
  features) can coexist with the MIT core.
